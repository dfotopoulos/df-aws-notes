### Step 1: Create an IAM Access Key

1. Log in to the [AWS Console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/).
2. In the top search bar, go to **IAM** &rarr; **Users**.
3. Choose your IAM user (or create one with `AdministratorAccess` or
   `AmazonEC2FullAccess`).
4. Click the **Security credentials** tab &rarr; scroll to **Access keys**
   &rarr; click **Create access key**.
5. Select **Command Line Interface (CLI)**, accept the tag recommendation, and
   click **Create access key**.
6. Copy the **Access key ID** and **Secret access key**.

### Step 2: Run `aws configure` in your terminal

Open your PowerShell terminal and run:

```powershell
aws configure
```

Fill in the prompts:

- **AWS Access Key ID**: _(paste your Access Key ID)_
- **AWS Secret Access Key**: _(paste your Secret Access Key)_
- **Default region name**: _(e.g. `us-east-1`, `eu-central-1`, or whatever
  region is selected in the top-right of your AWS Console)_
- **Default output format**: `json`
