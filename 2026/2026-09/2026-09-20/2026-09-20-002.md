Let's check the 3 essential items in the AWS Console:

### 1. Public IPv4 Address

1. Go to the
   [EC2 Instances Console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/ec2/home#Instances:).
2. Click on your Windows instance and check the **Details** pane at the bottom:
    - **Does it have a Public IPv4 address assigned?**
    - _(If it is blank, you need to attach an Elastic IP or launch into a public
      subnet)._

### 2. Inbound Port 3389 in Security Group

1. Click the **Security** tab below your instance details.
2. Click on the **Security group ID** &rarr; look at **Inbound rules**:
    - **Do you see a rule with Type `RDP` (Port `3389`, Protocol `TCP`)?**
    - _(If not, click **Edit inbound rules** &rarr; **Add rule** &rarr; Type:
      `RDP` &rarr; Source: `My IP` &rarr; Save)._

### 3. Key Pair (`.pem`) for Decrypting Password

1. In the EC2 console, select your instance and click the **Connect** button at
   the top right.
2. Select the **RDP client** tab &rarr; click **Get password**.
    - **Do you have the `.pem` file you selected/created when you launched the
      instance?**
